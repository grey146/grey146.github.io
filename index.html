Years spent refining a single joinery method before it entered production.

Craftsmanship

Slow furniture,
built to outlast trends.

Every piece begins as a sketch, not a spreadsheet. We work in solid timber and natural fibers, joined by hand using techniques chosen for strength rather than speed — so what you bring home is built to be repaired, not replaced.

01

Solid materials, no shortcuts

Solid walnut, oak, ash, and beech — never veneer over particleboard. What you see is what holds the piece together.

02

Joinery over hardware

Mortise-and-tenon and dovetail joints wherever the design allows, reducing reliance on glue and fasteners over time.

03

Small-batch production

Pieces are made in limited runs by a workshop of nine, so each one gets the attention a larger factory can't offer.
